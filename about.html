WHY WE
LOOK BACK.

Every explorer leaves a trace.

Exploring Explorers — ESquare is an interactive storytelling platform that brings NASA’s metal explorers to life. It follows the stories Viking, Opportunity, Perseverance, Cassini-Huygens, OSIRIS-REx, and Voyager through their destinations, discoveries, silences, and legacies. By presenting each spacecraft as an explorer with its own journey, ESquare makes space science more emotional, accessible, and memorable—showing that every mission leaves a story behind.

NASA’s missions are often remembered for their destinations: Mars, Saturn, Bennu, and interstellar space. But behind every discovery is an explorer—a rover, orbiter, lander, or probe designed to go where humans cannot.

ESquare is a storytelling archive about those objects and the missions behind them. It looks at what we left, why it is there, and what each object tells us.

This is an educational project. Mission details are simplified for storytelling; individual mission files should be checked against their linked scientific sources before publication.
