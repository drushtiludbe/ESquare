ABOUT THIS PROJECT / TRANSMISSION 000

WHY WE
LOOK BACK.

Every explorer leaves a trace.

Humanity has sent machines to places we may not visit for generations. They have travelled across deserts, seen distant asteroids, studied the Sun and travelled into interstellar space.

When a mission ends, its equipment does not always come home. A lander may remain on a moon. A rover may fall silent on Mars. A spacecraft may continue travelling long after its original assignment is complete.

ESquare is a storytelling archive about those objects and the missions behind them. It looks at what we left, why it is there, and what each object tells us.

This is an educational project. Mission details are simplified for storytelling; individual mission files should be checked against their linked scientific sources before publication.
